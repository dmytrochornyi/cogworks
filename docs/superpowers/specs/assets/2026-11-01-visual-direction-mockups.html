Which mood should Cogworks lean into?

Rough top-down sketches of the same little factory corner: ground, a belt, a machine, lamp poles, the HUD and hotbar. Colours and mood only, not final art.

A · Lantern market at dusk

Lavender-blue evening light, red paper lanterns on the lamp poles, red and teal roofs. The UI looks like cream paper with lacquer-red trim. Cozy and glowing.

B · Sunlit spirit forest

Lush, bright greens, mossy roofs on wooden machines, dappled sunlight and floating glowing motes. The UI looks like warm carved wood with leaf-green edges.

C · Patchwork castle meadow

Pastel flower meadow, machines that look patched together from mismatched pastel panels, brass bits and puffy chimney smoke. The UI looks like a quilt with stitched borders.

Worth knowing

The game already has a day/night cycle. A mix could work well: sunny forest or meadow by day (B or C), turning into the lantern market at dusk (A) as the lamps come on.
